How it works

Three minutes to set up. Then it runs quietly.

DueRadar is a tracker, not a payment app. It never asks for a card number, a MoMo PIN, or a bank password. You tell it the dates; it tells you when they are close.

  1. 1

    Add what you already pay

    A name, an amount, and the next date. Weekly, monthly, yearly, or every N days.

  2. 2

    Choose your nudge

    Bills get 7 and 1 day reminders. Trials get 3 and 1. Change it per item.

  3. 3

    Mark it paid and move on

    The next date rolls forward automatically. Your history stays for exports and review.
